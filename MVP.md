# V1 MVP

## Goal

Answer: “What reusable flat-rate amount should this individual HVAC repair task carry in my price book?”

## Included

- Book time and burdened-technician or shop labor cost basis
- Parts/material cost with markup or direct selling-price logic
- Fixed or percentage overhead and callback reserve
- Target-margin or markup pricing
- Standard, member, and after-hours prices
- Internal cost, gross profit, gross margin, and markup outputs
- Versioned browser-local draft recovery
- SEO guide, formula, worked example, mistakes, FAQs, canonical, sitemap, robots, and structured data
- Privacy-safe tool_visit and tool_complete analytics

## Success criteria

Cent-safe deterministic math, accessible mobile-first interaction, clear separation from service-call pricing, successful tests/lint/build, and a production deployment.
