# Scope

## In scope

One browser-local pricing scenario for an individual HVAC repair task: book time, internal labor basis, parts cost and selling logic, overhead allocation, callback reserve, target margin or markup, member discount, after-hours surcharge, and profitability results.

## Explicitly out of scope

- Trip, diagnostic, or minimum service-call fee calculation
- Customer records or task descriptions
- Multi-item price-book management
- Quotes, invoices, payments, billing, dispatch, or inventory
- Regional price data or AI price recommendations
- HVAC diagnostics, engineering, sizing, or code compliance
- Accounts, login, or cloud history

The app remains a repair-task pricing calculator, not service management software.
